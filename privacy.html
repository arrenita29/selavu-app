Selavu

Privacy note

Last updated 7 November 2026 · version v1-2026-11

Selavu turns your bank SMS into expense entries and keeps your balance up to date. This note lists exactly what it stores, why, and how you can delete it.

What Selavu stores

  • Your account: name, email, and your password (stored only in scrambled form by our login provider, never readable).
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: phone number, city, whether you're a student or working, your main bank, and how you heard about Selavu.
  • Your entries: amount, spent or received, who it was paid to, category, date and time, the balance your bank reported, and the bank's name.
  • Your phone tokens: a label like "iPhone" and when it was last used. The token itself is stored only in scrambled form.
  • Activity: when you last opened the app and the kind of device (iPhone, Android or computer).
  • Bank SMS Selavu couldn't read: only the bank's name and the time, so we know which bank formats to fix.
  • Your consent: that you're 18 or older and when you agreed to this note.

What Selavu never stores

  • The text of your bank SMS. Selavu reads it, keeps the details above, and throws the message away. Only a one-way fingerprint of it is kept, which can't be turned back into the text, so the same SMS isn't added twice.
  • Card numbers, full account numbers, OTPs, UPI PINs, Aadhaar or PAN.
  • Your contacts, location or other messages.

Why

  • To show your expenses and balance, and warn you when the balance is low.
  • To add entries automatically from your phone.
  • To understand how Selavu is used (sign-ups, active users, which banks to support) and improve it.
  • To contact you about your account if needed.

Selavu does not sell your data, show ads, or share your details with anyone else.

Who can see it

  • You see everything in your account.
  • The Selavu owner can see your name, email, profile details, when you joined and were last active, your device type, and how many entries you have. The owner cannot see your amounts, who you paid, or your balance.
  • Nobody else. Every customer's data is locked to their own account.

Where it's kept and for how long

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, over an encrypted connection. It's kept until you delete your account.

Your choices

  • See and change your details any time in the app, under "Your profile".
  • Delete your account in "Your profile". This immediately and permanently removes your account and every entry, token and profile detail.
  • Withdraw your consent by deleting your account. It's as easy as signing up.
  • Selavu is only for people aged 18 and older.

Questions or complaints

Email arrenita29@gmail.com. You'll get a reply within 7 days. If this note changes, you'll be asked to agree to the new version the next time you open the app.
